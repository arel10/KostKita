## Brand & Style

The design system projects trust, residential warmth, and disciplined SaaS efficiency for the Indonesian proptech ecosystem. It bridges two distinct psychological mindsets: the aspirational, comfort-seeking tenant browsing urban living spaces (*Anak Kost*), and the meticulous, yield-focused property owner (*Juragan Kost*). 

The aesthetic is **Tropical Modernist SaaS**—combining clean architectural structure, generous white space, and rich organic tones inspired by modern Indonesian urban spaces. Surfaces are light, crisp, and breathable, grounding dense operational workflows in effortless usability. Glass elements are restricted to floating navigation overlays and map controllers; the core interface relies on layered card structures, tailored micro-borders, and tactile interaction surfaces.

The tone is approachable yet authoritative, establishing institutional reliability for billing and contracts while remaining hospitable and warm during room discovery and tenant onboarding.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by deep tropical botany, sunlight accents, and high-legibility slate neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Forest Teal - `#0D5C4D`):** Represents security, property longevity, and trust. Applied to primary CTAs, active navigation indicators, brand anchors, and high-priority metrics. Hover variant is `#084035`, with soft container fills utilizing `#E8F5F1`.
- **Secondary (Terracotta Amber - `#D97706`):** Evokes Indonesian teak warmth, golden hour sunlight, and hospitality. Applied to premium property tiering, ratings, alerts requiring attention, and highlighted amenities. Supporting shade: `#F59E0B`; container fill: `#FEF3C7`.
- **Utility / Tertiary (WhatsApp Action Green - `#25D366`):** Reserved exclusively for localized communication CTAs, instant tenant chat actions, and owner-to-tenant direct confirmations.
- **Vibrant Accent (Emerald Bright - `#10B981`):** Represents verified statuses, positive occupancy deltas, and completed payments.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- Canvas Background: `#F8FAFC`
- Surface Raised: `#FFFFFF`
- Surface Secondary / Inactive Tracks: `#F1F5F9`
- Subtle Divider: `#E2E8F0`
- Slate Muted: `#94A3B8`
- Body Text: `#334155`
- High-Contrast Headings & Ink: `#0F172A`

### Status Tones
- **Paid / Active:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `#A7F3D0`
- **Pending / Inactive:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border `#FDE68A`
- **Overdue / Suspended:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `#FECACA`
- **Draft / Neutral:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `#CBD5E1`

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is utilized across all layers to infuse a contemporary, clean, and locally contextual typographic foundation. 

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- Numerical data in dashboards (Indonesian Rupiah formats, e.g., `Rp 2.450.000`) must utilize tabular figures (`tnum`) with weights of 600 or 700 to ensure aligned scanning in room availability lists and billing logs.
- Discovery headings leverage tight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to maintain an editorial, premium look across property titles.
- Operational metadata, badges, and super-admin audit logs use `label-sm` with slight uppercase tracking (`0.02em`) for clarity under compact heights.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an 8pt base grid for spacing and component sizing, pairing tight data structures in the operational panels with airy breathing space on public pages.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Canvas margin `1rem` (`margin-sm`), gutter `1rem` (`gutter-sm`). Bottom bar navigation anchors primary actions on discovery screens. Metric widgets collapse to single-column swipeable cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Canvas margin `1.5rem`, gutter `1.5rem`. Side-by-side room split views and dynamic bottom sheets for room reservation forms.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout. Max container constraint of `1360px` for discovery, and full-width fluid layouts for dashboard management consoles. Margin `3rem` (`margin-lg`), gutter `2rem` (`gutter-lg`).

### Density Modes
- **Discovery Mode:** Relaxed rhythm (`space-lg` card gaps, `space-xl` section margins).
- **Dashboard / Admin Mode:** Compact data density (`space-sm` to `space-md` element spacing, `0.75rem` vertical row paddings) for higher visual throughput.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through fine-tuned ambient shadows combined with delicate slate boundaries. Pure black shadows are prohibited; all depth layers inherit an ambient forest-slate tint (`hsla(215, 28%, 17%, ...)`).

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Base background for pages and data dashboards.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Property Cards):** `#FFFFFF`, hairline boundary `1px solid #E2E8F0`, layered shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(13, 92, 77, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, border shifted to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdown Menus, Popovers, Floating Date Pickers):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Sheets, Payment Drawers, Verification Inspect Panels):** Scrim `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`; card elevated with `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(13, 92, 77, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The roundedness level is set to **2 (Rounded)**, producing structural softness without sacrificing professional dashboard credibility.

- Buttons, form controls, and base input fields: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Internal cards, room availability tiles, metric widgets: `1rem` (16px, `rounded-lg`).
- High-level containers, modal sheets, and featured property cards: `1.5rem` (24px, `rounded-xl`).
- Status tags, micro-badges, and avatars: `9999px` (fully circular / pill).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0D5C4D`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`. Smooth hover to `#084035`. Focus ring of `2px` with `#10B981` offset by `2px`.
- **Secondary / Subtle:** Surface `#E8F5F1`, text `#0D5C4D`, border `1px solid transparent`. Hover shifts background to `#D1EBE3`.
- **WhatsApp Action:** `#25D366` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold `label-md`. Elevates on hover with ambient emerald glow.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#334155`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Input Fields
- Base height `44px` on desktop and mobile for comfortable touch targets.
- Border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, surface `#FFFFFF`. Focus transitions border to `#0D5C4D` and adds an outer shadow ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 92, 77, 0.12)`.
- Currency prefix inputs display fixed `Rp` adornment in `#64748B` with divider.

### Status Badges
- Constructed with pill radius (`9999px`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Text rendered in `label-sm` with an integrated 6px circular dot indicator.
- Semantic configurations:
  - *Active / Terverifikasi / Paid:* Tint `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot `#10B981`.
  - *Pending / Menunggu Pembayaran:* Tint `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, dot `#F59E0B`.
  - *Overdue / Ditolak / Suspended:* Tint `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, dot `#EF4444`.
  - *Draft / Kosong:* Tint `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, dot `#94A3B8`.

### Property Cards (Public Discovery)
- Ratio 4:3 image carousel with top-right favorite heart button (floating frosted surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- Gender tag chip (Campur, Putra, Putri) pinned top-left with distinct micro-colors.
- Bottom section displays bold localized pricing (`Rp X.XXX.XXX / bulan`), walk-score or distance to key campuses/offices, and current vacancy count.

### Metric Cards & Room Grids (Owner Dashboard)
- **Metric Cards:** `#FFFFFF` background, subtle border, containing numeric metric (`currency-stat`), 30-day delta indicator chip, and mini sparkline.
- **Room Grid Tiles:** Modular visual representation of property floor layouts. Color-coded borders: Available (Forest Green), Occupied (Slate), Maintenance (Amber). Displays Room Number, Tenant Name snippet, and billing status indicator.

### Data Tables (Super Admin)
- Striped or bordered rows with `0.75rem` vertical cell padding.
- Header row styled in `label-sm` with background `#F8FAFC` and text `#64748B`.
- Integrated instant filter chips (KTP Pending, Kost Baru, Payout Request).
